Three steps, no new habits

Cairn sits alongside the pipeline you already trust — it doesn't ask your team to change how they ship.

01

Ship

Connect Cairn to GitHub Actions, CircleCI, or ArgoCD in a few minutes. It listens for deploy events — nothing about your pipeline changes.

02

Watch

From the moment a release goes live, Cairn correlates it against error rate, latency, and saturation from the tools you already use — Datadog, Prometheus, or your own metrics endpoint.

03

Decide

You get a verdict, not a wall of graphs: hold, watch, or safe. If a release turns bad, Cairn tells you which commit did it and how to roll back — before your on-call finds out at 2am.
